The six steps I still use

There's a six-step process I wrote down in 2018 that I still use: Listen, Research, Strategize, Execute, Evaluate, Growth.

It isn't a slogan. It's the order of the work.

  • Listen for the job that isn't getting done, and write it back in the other person's words.
  • Research what's already public — the site, the emails, the product — and keep facts separate from hope. If a number isn't measured, it doesn't go in the plan.
  • Strategize by picking one path for this season and saying what you will not do.
  • Execute the path: the page, the offer, the follow-up. Something a person can actually use.
  • Evaluate honestly. Did people take the next step? If not, where did they stop?
  • Growth is turning the volume up only after that path works, then listening again.

I still use this on Gil Marketing work. It doesn't name clients and it doesn't promise a result. It just keeps the work in the right order.
