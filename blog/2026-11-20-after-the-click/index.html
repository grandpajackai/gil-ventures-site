I wouldn’t start with ads.

Ads buy attention. They don’t fix the path after the click.

If I were starting a DTC brand this quarter, I wouldn’t start with ads.

Ads buy attention. They don’t fix what happens after the click. If the offer is fuzzy, the page tells a different story than the ad, or nobody wrote the follow-up, more spend just sends more people into the same fog.

I’d get four things straight first:

  1. Make the offer obvious. One promise, one next step.
  2. Make the page continue the line that earned the visit.
  3. Write the follow-up — welcome, then help — before buying more traffic.
  4. Watch the next step (add to cart, subscribe, or bounce) and fix leaks before raising spend.

Only then would I buy more traffic.

This is a sequence, not a case study. I’m not attaching results I haven’t measured.
