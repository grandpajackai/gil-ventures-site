Questions founders ask

Is three weeks really enough?

Yes, if we start by Nov 6. The timeline above has a full week between first drafts (Nov 20) and go-live (Nov 24) for your edits and testing. That's also why kickoffs close Nov 6 and we cap the season at 8 brands.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify stores, Klaviyo for email and SMS, and Meta and Google for ads. On Launch + Run we can also run TikTok Shop and Amazon ads. On a different email platform? Ask on the call and we'll tell you straight whether we can do it in time.

Do you write in our brand voice?

Yes. Kickoff covers your voice, past winners and anything off-limits, and you approve every draft before it's scheduled.

What do you need from us?

A kickoff call, collaborator access to Shopify, your email/SMS platform and ad accounts, product and brand assets, and one round of edits by Nov 23.

Are you going to make us discount everything?

No. We start with your margins. Plenty of brands win BFCM with gift sets, bundles, free gifts or early access instead of a sitewide discount.

You do. The emails, flows, sale page and creative are built in your accounts and stay yours after the season.

What happens after Cyber Monday?
